Same map. Different mission.

The Spike is the device attackers carry. Once it's planted, the round becomes a race against a new clock.

Attackers

Take the site

Plant the Spike. Protect it until it detonates.

  1. Carry the Spike to a marked plant site. Clear space with your team before planting.
  2. Press 4 to equip the Spike, then hold fire to plant. Stay still for the full 4 seconds.
  3. After planting, watch routes to the Spike. You don't need to hunt every defender.

Defenders

Hold the site

Stop the plant. If it goes down, defuse it.

  1. Cover entry routes and gather information. If attackers never plant, a timer win is yours.
  2. If a site falls, regroup and retake together. Clear danger before touching the Spike.
  3. Hold your Use key (default 4) near the Spike to defuse. The halfway checkpoint saves progress.

Before the plant, eliminating the enemy team wins. After the plant, defenders still have to defuse.

Good habits beat fast hands.

You don't need highlight-reel aim. Start with these three habits and give yourself easier shots.

01

Stop, then shoot.

Most guns lose accuracy while you're moving. Release your movement keys and settle before firing.

02

Keep your crosshair ready.

Aim at likely head height, close to the corner an enemy could appear from, not at the floor.

03

Burst. Reset. Repeat.

At medium or long range, fire a few shots, pause, and readjust. Long sprays get harder to control.

One more thing: hold Shift to walk quietly. Running is faster, but your footsteps give away information.

You don't need every callout on day one. Learn the sites, the routes between them, and where your teammates are.

Check the minimap.

Glance when you're safe. Notice teammates, revealed enemies, and which routes aren't covered.

Rotate with a reason.

A rotation is moving to help another site. Use confirmed information; don't abandon a site at the first sound.

A useful callout sounds like

Two A main. Spike spotted.

Where + how many + what you know. No mic? Ping the map. Keep it kind, short, and factual.

Speak a little VALORANT.

Heard something unfamiliar? These are a few words you'll hear from teammates. Now you're in on it.

Eco

A low-spend round to save credits for stronger gear later.

Trade

Eliminate the enemy who just eliminated your teammate.

Rotate

Move from one part of the map to help at another.

Utility

A catch-all word for agent abilities, like smokes and flashes.

Peek

Expose yourself to check or challenge an angle.

Angle

A line of sight where someone might be watching.

Flank

Approach the enemy from the side or behind.

Clutch

Win a round as the last player alive on your team.
